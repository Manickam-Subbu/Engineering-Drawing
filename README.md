## Abstract

This project presents the design and fabrication of a multipurpose wooden desk
organizer built from wooden boards and blocks.

The organizer combines a decorative clock-holder panel fitted with a miniature
analog clock insert, partition panels forming multiple storage compartments,
and an angled support panel that also functions as a stand.

The base board acts as the structural foundation. The panels are fixed using
wood adhesive and reinforcing screws.

The project includes both orthographic and pictorial/isometric representations
to show the arrangement and three-dimensional form of the organizer.

## Working Principle

The base board provides the foundation for the complete organizer.

A clock holder panel is positioned at one end of the base. It contains a
circular cavity into which the miniature analog clock is fitted.

Partition panels are fixed vertically along the base to form separate
compartments for storing pens, stationery and small accessories.

An angled support panel is positioned at the opposite end. It provides
additional stability and can also be used as a stand for a phone, tablet or
small frame.

The components are joined using wood adhesive and reinforced with screws
where required.

---

## Fabrication Procedure

### Step 1 – Material Selection

A well-seasoned wooden board and block, free from major knots or cracks, are
selected for the base and clock holder panel.

### Step 2 – Marking and Measurement

The required dimensions of the base board, partition panels, support panel
and clock holder profile are marked using a try square, marking gauge and
pencil.

### Step 3 – Cutting

The marked wooden pieces are cut to size using a tenon saw.

The decorative eared profile of the clock holder panel is also cut along the
marked outline.

### Step 4 – Shaping

The cut edges and decorative profile are shaped and smoothed using a chisel
and mallet.

### Step 5 – Clock Cavity Preparation

A circular cavity is prepared in the clock holder panel to accommodate the
analog clock insert.

### Step 6 – Joint Preparation

The mating surfaces of the base board and panels are prepared and checked for
a close and flat fit.

### Step 7 – Assembly

The clock holder panel, partition panels and support panel are fixed to the
base board using wood adhesive.

Wood screws are used to reinforce the joints where required.

### Step 8 – Clock Fitting

The analog clock insert is fitted into the prepared circular cavity and
secured in place.

### Step 9 – Finishing

The complete assembly is sanded to remove rough edges.

A wood finish or varnish is then applied to protect the surface.

### Step 10 – Final Inspection

The completed organizer is checked for:

- Stability
- Panel alignment
- Compartment arrangement
- Clock fitting
- Surface finish

## Maintenance

- Wipe the surface periodically using a dry or slightly damp cloth.
- Avoid prolonged exposure to moisture.
- Check glued joints periodically.
- Replace the clock battery when required.
- Re-apply wood polish or varnish periodically.
- Keep the organizer away from prolonged direct sunlight and heat.

---

## Safety Precautions

- Use saws and chisels carefully.
- Always cut away from the body.
- Wear safety goggles while cutting, sanding or chiseling.
- Clamp the workpiece securely before cutting.
- Keep fingers away from blades and sharp edges.
- Store tools safely after use.

## Conclusion

The project presents a practical wooden desk organizer combining functional
storage with a decorative analog clock.

The organizer consists of a base board, clock holder panel, partition panels,
support/stand panel and analog clock insert.

The base board provides the structural foundation, while the partition panels
create storage compartments and the support panel provides additional
stability.

The project demonstrates basic carpentry operations including marking,
cutting, shaping, jointing, assembly and finishing.

It also provides practical exposure to the tools and techniques used in
Engineering Practices Laboratory carpentry work.
